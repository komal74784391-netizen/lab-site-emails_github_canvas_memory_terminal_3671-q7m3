---
title: "Adaptive Graph Coarsening with Learned Pooling Operators"
authors: "Angela Nguyen, Ravi Okafor, Tomas Lindqvist"
venue: "NeurIPS 2025"
status: "accepted"
year: 2025
---

We introduce a family of differentiable coarsening operators whose pooling schedule is learned jointly with the downstream task. Experiments on molecular and citation graphs show consistent gains over fixed pooling baselines at equal parameter budgets.
